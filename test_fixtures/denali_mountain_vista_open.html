Current Conditions - Denali National Park & Preserve

Fall/Winter 2026

In the fall, weather permitting, the road is open for personal vehicles to drive to Teklanika (mile 30).

The road may close at Mountain Vista (mile 13) or Park Headquarters (mile 3) at any time due to winter weather.

Conditions on the Denali Park Road

Some park operations are altered due to the closure of the Denali Park Road at Mile 43.
