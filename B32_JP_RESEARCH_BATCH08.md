## jp-017 — 金澤兼六園 / Kenroku-en

### Evidence

Official Kenroku-en sources:
- Kotoji-toro (徽軫灯籠) is explicitly described as a representative symbol and an optimal commemorative-photo spot.
- The official photo-guide route begins with Kotoji-toro and also lists the observation deck, Karasaki pine, Horai island, and Hanamibashi.
- The garden is open year-round with seasonal paid hours and separate early-morning free-entry windows.
- Last-entry times are explicitly published.

Sources:
- https://kenrokuen.or.jp/highlight/
- https://kenrokuen.or.jp/photo-guide/
- https://kenrokuen.or.jp/information/

Camera-zone cross-check:
- a photographed Kotoji-toro / Kasumigaike viewpoint and the prefectural live-camera area converge around approximately 36.56337, 136.66283.

### Candidate Opportunity

**jp-017-P01 — 徽軫燈籠・霞之池經典庭園景觀**
- visible-light / landscape baseline.
- Camera Zone: Kotoji-toro / Kasumigaike public path area.
- best time: official open-access windows in JST.
- required: lantern / pond / garden context readable, adequate visibility, no severe precipitation/fog.
- boosters: calm water and seasonal foliage/snow scenery may improve the scene, but are not required.
- access: use official seasonal early-entry + paid-entry windows; recommendation uses published last-entry times.
- runtime: minimum-sufficient visibility.

## jp-018 — 名古屋城 / Nagoya Castle

### Evidence

Official Nagoya Castle:
- normal opening: 09:00–16:30.
- Main Tower Keep is currently not enterable.
- official site highlights Honmaru Palace, surviving turrets and seasonal vegetation as primary sights.
- some 2026 events extend opening into the evening, but only on explicit dates.

Sources:
- https://www.nagoyajo.city.nagoya.jp/
- https://www.nagoyajo.city.nagoya.jp/event/2026/08/20260826_4914.html

### Migration decision

Keep, but normal runtime should begin with a daytime castle-ground Opportunity only.
Do not create a year-round night Opportunity from event-only illumination periods.
Exact exterior Camera Zone should be verified before activation.

## jp-023 — 京都清水寺 / Kiyomizu-dera

### Evidence

Official Kiyomizu-dera:
- normal opening begins at 06:00; closing varies by season/events.
- 2026 schedule includes limited spring/summer night special-viewing periods.
- personal visitor photography is compatible with ordinary worship/visitation, but drones, monopods and tripods are prohibited.
- commercial shooting, model/cosplay/wedding shoots, long stationary shoots and obstruction of visitors are prohibited.

Sources:
- https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/access/
- https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/news/etiquette-inside-the-temple-grounds.php
- https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/en/faq/

### Migration decision

Keep, but only as a handheld visitor-photography Opportunity with explicit etiquette metadata.
Do not create a generic year-round night Opportunity; special night viewing is date-specific.
Exact Camera Zone / composition should be researched before runtime activation.
