## us-006 Bryce Canyon National Park

Official sources:
- NPS Bryce FAQ: https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/faqs.htm
- NPS Bryce viewpoints: https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/viewpoints.htm
- NPS Sunrise Point: https://www.nps.gov/places/sunrise-point.htm
- NPS Stargazing at Bryce: https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/stargazing-at-bryce-canyon.htm
- NPS operating hours: https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/hours.htm

Verified subject facts:
- Bryce Amphitheater viewpoints are strongly east-facing.
- NPS identifies Sunrise Point and Bryce Point as popular sunrise choices.
- NPS recommends Inspiration Point or Paria View for sunset light rather than assuming “Sunset Point” faces sunset.
- Bryce is an International Dark Sky Park; NPS directly recommends Sunrise Point, Sunset Point, Inspiration Point and Paria View for stargazing, and Queen's Garden / Navajo Trail for hoodoo foreground night photography.
- The park is open 24/7 year-round, but temporary snow closures can affect roads.

Planned Opportunities:
1. Bryce Amphitheater sunrise / warm morning hoodoo layers — directional horizon + visibility.
2. Inspiration Point / Paria View sunset light on hoodoos — directional horizon + visibility.
3. Hoodoo foreground + Milky Way / dark sky — astronomy ephemeris.

Access note:
- Main park is 24h, but winter road/viewpoint closures must remain a current-condition caveat; do not hard-code all viewpoints as always reachable.

## us-007 Zion National Park

Official sources:
- NPS Sunrise and Sunset in Zion: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/sunrise-and-sunset.htm
- NPS Canyon Overlook: https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-canyon-overlook.htm
- NPS operating hours: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/hours.htm

Verified subject facts:
- NPS explicitly recommends Canyon Overlook Trail for sunrise light entering Zion Canyon.
- NPS explicitly recommends Pa'rus Trail for sunset/golden light on the Watchman.
- Human History Museum patio is also an official sunrise location.
- Canyon Overlook parking is extremely limited.
- Zion is open 24/7 year-round; shuttle operation changes vehicle access to Zion Canyon Scenic Drive.

Planned Opportunities:
1. Canyon Overlook sunrise / Towers and Temples illumination — directional horizon + visibility.
2. Pa'rus Trail / Watchman sunset — directional horizon + visibility.

Access note:
- Do not equate park-open-24h with unlimited vehicle access. Shuttle/road rules and temporary closures remain access facts.
- Ordinary personal visitor photography is not modeled from commercial filming permit zones.

Planned Opportunities:
1. Glacier Point / Washburn Point Half Dome panorama — visibility + dynamic access.
2. Glacier Point Half Dome night sky — astronomy ephemeris + dynamic access.

Access note:
- Do not encode “late May–Oct/Nov” as guaranteed fixed dates.
- Until a current Yosemite road-status provider is integrated, Glacier Point vehicle-dependent Opportunities should fail closed or remain module-pending.
- The Place name “Half Dome” does not mean the Camera Zone is the Half Dome summit.

## us-010 Yellowstone Grand Prismatic Spring

Official sources:
- NPS Yellowstone photography: https://www.nps.gov/yell/planyourvisit/photography.htm
- NPS Grand Prismatic Spring: https://www.nps.gov/places/000/grand-prismatic-spring.htm
- NPS Grand Prismatic Overlook Trail: https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/yell-trail-grand-prismatic-overlook.htm
- NPS Midway Geyser Basin: https://www.nps.gov/places/000/midway-geyser-basin-trailhead.htm
- NPS Yellowstone thermal-basin safety: https://www.nps.gov/yell/planyourvisit/thermal-basin-exploring.htm
- NPS current/backcountry conditions: https://www.nps.gov/yell/planyourvisit/situationreport.htm

Verified subject facts:
- NPS directly identifies Grand Prismatic as a major photography subject.
- The Fairy Falls trail leads to the only elevated overlook.
- Midway Geyser Basin boardwalk provides the legal ground-level view.
- Off-trail travel is prohibited; thermal-ground safety is a hard gate.
- Parking is limited.
- Access/trail/road conditions can be seasonal or temporarily closed.

Planned Opportunities:
1. Grand Prismatic elevated overlook — dynamic access + local/visibility quality.
2. Midway boardwalk ground-level color / steam composition — dynamic access + local-scene quality.

Model caution:
- Do not invent a “best steam temperature” rule from generic intuition.
- Coarse meteorological visibility is not a complete proxy for near-spring steam obscuration.
- If no trustworthy thermal-steam visibility contract is available, use conservative local-scene scoring and/or keep the higher-confidence elevated composition pending behind access/current-condition state.
